Dubai & UAE Travel Guide

Discover Dubai and the United Arab Emirates — a destination of modern cities, luxury experiences, beautiful beaches, desert adventures and rich Arabian culture.

Discover Dubai & UAE

The United Arab Emirates combines spectacular modern architecture, beautiful beaches, traditional Arabian culture and unforgettable desert experiences. From the iconic skyline of Dubai to the cultural heritage of Abu Dhabi and the natural beauty of Ras Al Khaimah, the UAE offers something for every type of traveler.

Popular Cities

🏙️ Dubai

Dubai is famous for its spectacular skyline, luxury shopping, world-class hotels, beaches, entertainment and iconic attractions such as Burj Khalifa and Dubai Marina.

🕌 Abu Dhabi

Abu Dhabi is the capital of the UAE and is known for its grand mosques, cultural attractions, waterfront, museums and beautiful city landscape.

🏛️ Sharjah

Sharjah is known for its museums, traditional architecture, cultural attractions and family-friendly destinations. It is an important cultural center of the UAE.

⛰️ Ras Al Khaimah

Ras Al Khaimah offers mountains, beaches, desert landscapes and outdoor adventures. It is a great destination for travelers looking for nature and adventure.

Top Attractions

  • 🏙️ Visit the Burj Khalifa and enjoy views of Dubai.
  • 🕌 Explore Sheikh Zayed Grand Mosque in Abu Dhabi.
  • 🏖️ Relax on the beautiful beaches of Dubai and Abu Dhabi.
  • 🏜️ Experience a traditional UAE desert safari.
  • 🛍️ Explore Dubai Mall and the UAE's famous shopping destinations.
  • 🏛️ Discover museums and cultural attractions in Sharjah.
  • ⛰️ Explore the Hajar Mountains and outdoor attractions around Ras Al Khaimah.

Food & Culture

Emirati cuisine reflects the traditions of the Arabian Peninsula. Visitors can enjoy dishes such as machboos, harees, luqaimat and grilled meats, as well as a huge variety of international cuisine. Traditional souks, Arabic coffee, dates and local hospitality are also an important part of the UAE travel experience.

Dubai & UAE Travel Tips

  • 🛂 Check UAE visa and entry requirements before traveling.
  • 🌡️ Check the weather, especially during the hot summer months.
  • 👕 Dress respectfully when visiting religious and cultural places.
  • 🕌 Follow local customs and rules when visiting mosques.
  • 💳 Keep a payment card and some UAE Dirhams available.
  • 📱 Keep your phone, maps and important travel information accessible.
  • 🚕 Use licensed taxis or trusted transportation services.
  • 🏨 Book hotels early during major holidays and peak travel periods.
