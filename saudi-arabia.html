Saudi Arabia Travel Guide

Saudi Arabia offers a unique combination of Islamic heritage, ancient history, modern cities, deserts, mountains and coastal destinations. From Makkah and Madinah to Riyadh, Jeddah and AlUla, there are many places to explore.

Popular Saudi Foods

Try some of the most popular traditional foods and drinks.

Kabsa

Kabsa

Spiced rice traditionally served with chicken or meat.

Mandi

Mandi

Fragrant rice served with tender meat, often cooked with traditional spices.

Jareesh

Jareesh

A traditional dish made from crushed wheat with a soft and comforting texture.

Saleeg

Saleeg

A creamy rice dish traditionally associated with the western region.

Mutabbaq

Mutabbaq

A stuffed folded pancake-style dish commonly enjoyed as a snack.

Sambusa

Sambusa

Crispy pastry filled with meat, vegetables or cheese.
