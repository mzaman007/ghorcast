Makkah Travel Guide

A practical guide to visiting Makkah, one of the most important cities in the Islamic world and home to Masjid al-Haram.

How to Get to Makkah

Most international visitors arrive in Saudi Arabia through Jeddah and continue to Makkah by train, taxi, private car or bus.

  • Jeddah to Makkah by Haramain High-Speed Railway
  • Taxi or private transfer from Jeddah
  • Bus services are also available

Getting Around Makkah

Taxis, ride-hailing services, buses and hotel transportation are commonly used for getting around the city.

  • Taxi and ride-hailing services
  • Local buses
  • Hotel shuttle services
  • Walking around areas close to Masjid al-Haram

Where to Stay

Makkah has a wide range of accommodation, from luxury hotels near Masjid al-Haram to more affordable options farther away.

Visitors can enjoy traditional Saudi and Middle Eastern dishes throughout Makkah.

  • Kabsa

Travel Tips

  • Keep your passport and important documents secure.
  • Carry water, especially when walking outdoors.
  • Dress respectfully and follow local customs.
  • Check official travel and pilgrimage requirements before travelling.
  • Allow extra time when travelling during busy pilgrimage periods.
