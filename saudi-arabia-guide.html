Discover Saudi Arabia

Saudi Arabia offers a unique combination of Islamic heritage, historic destinations, modern cities, beautiful landscapes and traditional Arabian culture. From the holy cities of Makkah and Madinah to the modern attractions of Riyadh and Jeddah, there is something for every traveler.

Popular Cities

🕋 Makkah

Makkah is the holiest city in Islam and home to Masjid al-Haram and the Kaaba. Millions of Muslims visit the city every year for Umrah and Hajj.

🕌 Madinah

Madinah is the city of the Prophet Muhammad ﷺ and home to Al-Masjid an-Nabawi. It is one of the most important destinations for Muslim travelers.

🏙️ Riyadh

Riyadh is the capital of Saudi Arabia and a modern city known for shopping, restaurants, entertainment and impressive architecture.

🌊 Jeddah

Jeddah is a major coastal city on the Red Sea, famous for its waterfront, historic Al-Balad area, shopping and seafood.

Saudi Arabia Travel Tips

  • 🛂 Check visa and entry requirements before traveling.
  • 🌡️ Check the weather before planning outdoor activities.
  • 👕 Dress respectfully and follow local customs.
  • 🕌 If performing Umrah or Hajj, learn the relevant rituals beforehand.
  • 💳 Keep a payment card and some Saudi Riyals available.
  • 📱 Keep your phone, maps and important travel information accessible.
  • 🏨 Book accommodation early during busy pilgrimage seasons.
